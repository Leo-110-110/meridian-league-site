How the table is calculated

Three points for a win, one for a draw and none for a loss. Goals for and against are summed from final scores; goal difference is goals for minus goals against.

Ties

Clubs level on points are ordered by goal difference, then goals scored, then head-to-head points among the tied clubs, then club name.

Form

The five chips are each club's last five league matches, oldest first: a filled chip is a win, a pale chip a draw, an outlined chip a loss.
